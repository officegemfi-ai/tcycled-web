Two parts, kept apart.

The rules are coming in steps.

What is already in force, and what Germany is still deciding. tcycled records the weights and outcomes from the first bag on.

Today
  1. 1 Jan 2025Separate collectionEU countries must collect textiles separately from other waste.
  2. 16 Oct 2025Textile EPR becomes EU lawThe revised Waste Framework Directive enters into force.
  3. 27 Mar 2026Key points for GermanyThe environment ministry proposes competing producer schemes and a 70% collection target.
  4. 19 Jul 2026No destroying unsold clothesThe ESPR ban applies to large companies, returns included.
  5. 2 Mar 2027One reporting formatDiscarded unsold products are reported in a standard EU format.
  6. 17 Jun 2027National law dueMember states must have transposed the directive.
  7. 17 Apr 2028EPR in every member stateProducers pay for collecting and sorting the textiles they sell.
  8. 19 Jul 2030Medium-sized companies tooThe destruction ban extends to medium-sized companies.

Sources: EUWID European Commission BMUKN key points Trustrace VdA

One API for the take-back.

Add bags to orders, accept them at the till and hear about every step. Weights and outcomes come through artusNet.

  • POST /v1/bag-requestsAdd a tcycled bag to a customer's order.
  • POST /v1/own-box-labelsCreate a label for a customer's own box.
  • POST /v1/store-dropoffsAccept a bag at your checkout and issue the voucher.
  • GET /v1/bags/{code}Public bag status for your site and your app.
  • return.in_transit, dropoff.accepted, return.weighedWebhooks for your CRM, checkout and reporting.
