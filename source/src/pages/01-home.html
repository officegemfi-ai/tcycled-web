How it worksWhat goes in the bag

Your voucher arrives before your bag does.

You don't wait for the sorting. As soon as the carrier scans the bag for the first time, your voucher is in your wallet, or in your inbox if you registered the bag without the app.

Where your things really end up.

In Bad Oldesloe, at Artus. Every bag is weighed and sorted by hand, and you can follow yours all the way there.

For brands that need to prove their take-back.

By 17 April 2028 every EU country must have extended producer responsibility (EPR) for textiles in place. Germany has published key points; the law hasn't been passed yet.
