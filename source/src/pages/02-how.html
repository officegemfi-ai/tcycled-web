Questions, answered.

Does it cost anything?{{i:plus}}

No. The bag, the app and registering cost nothing. If you send the bag on its own, your shop's return terms apply.

How do I get a tcycled bag?{{i:plus}}

Ask for one when you order from a partner shop, at the checkout or in the app, and it comes in your parcel. No bag yet? Use your own box with a label from the app.

Do I need the app?{{i:plus}}

No. Register the bag on this page with your email, and the voucher comes by email. The app shows every step and keeps all your vouchers in one wallet.

What is my voucher worth?{{i:plus}}

Your shop sets the value and the conditions. You see both as soon as the bag is registered, before you send it.

When do I get my voucher?{{i:plus}}

As soon as the carrier scans your bag for the first time, or when store staff scan the cap. Usually that's the day you send it.

My clothes have holes. Can I still send them?{{i:plus}}

Yes, as long as they're clean and dry. What can't be worn again is recycled where possible; what can't be recycled goes to energy recovery. Dirty things belong in the household bin.

Can I send shoes?{{i:plus}}

Yes, tied together in pairs. A single shoe can't be worn again, so please keep those out.

Where do my clothes go?{{i:plus}}

To Artus in Bad Oldesloe, where every bag is weighed and sorted by hand. See what happens next.

What are Loops?{{i:plus}}

You get 1 Loop for every bag that arrives. Spend three Loops for €5 off a product made with recycled fibre.
